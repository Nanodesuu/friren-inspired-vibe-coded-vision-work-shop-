Two-hand spell · 3D

◉ Parallax Orb

Hold both hands apart at chest height and pinch your thumb and index finger on each hand. Keep both pinches held. Spread your hands to grow the orb, tilt the line between them to turn it, and move both hands toward the camera to bring it forward. To throw, move both hands and release either pinch while they’re still moving. Push toward the webcam to send the orb closer and make it grow; pull both hands back toward your chest to send it away and make it shrink. Combine either with a sideways flick for a diagonal throw. Release while still to dissolve it.

Two-hand spell · 3D charge

⟲ Aether Coil

Show both open palms to the camera, hold them apart and still for about one second, then pinch with both hands to summon a charged orb. The charge is spent on your next throw, which leaves a golden trail and a larger burst.
